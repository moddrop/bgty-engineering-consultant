Who We Are

Thai Engineering. International Standards.

บริษัท บีจีทีวาย เอ็นจิเนียริ่ง แอนด์ คอนซัลแทนต์ จำกัด — registered and operating out of Bangkok's Huai Khwang district — was founded with a clear mandate: bring world-class engineering consultancy to Thailand's rapidly evolving construction sector.

We specialize in technical engineering works and consultancy, combining the rigor of international engineering practice with deep knowledge of the Thai regulatory landscape. Our multilingual team — fluent in Thai, Chinese, and English — bridges communication gaps that often derail complex joint-venture projects.

Our Rama 9 office serves as the hub for project management, design coordination, and client advisory across Bangkok and upcountry sites.
