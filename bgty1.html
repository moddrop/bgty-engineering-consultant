Who We Are关于我们

A Bangkok firm built for complex projects. 专为复杂项目而生的曼谷工程公司

We're a registered engineering and consultancy firm based on Rama 9 Road in Bangkok's Huai Khwang district. We were set up in 2018 to serve the steady stream of construction projects in Bangkok that needed proper engineering support — especially ones involving Chinese contractors or joint ventures.

Our team handles structural design, construction supervision, MEP coordination, and technical document translation. We also have dedicated interpreters who work on-site, embedded in the project team.

We work Monday to Friday, 8am–5pm, and travel to sites when the project requires it.

公司位于曼谷惠旺县拉玛九路，于2018年正式注册成立，专为当地大量需要专业工程支持的建设项目提供服务，尤其擅长服务涉及中国承包商或合资方的项目。

我们的团队负责结构设计、施工监督、机电工程协调及技术文件翻译。公司亦有专职口译员驻场，融入项目团队协同工作。

办公时间为周一至周五上午8时至下午5时，如项目有需要，我们亦可赴外地工地。

What We Do服务范围

Our Services 核心服务

01
Structural & Civil Engineering结构与土木工程

Structural analysis, foundation design, and civil works for residential, commercial, and industrial projects. We work to both Thai standards and international codes.为住宅、商业及工业项目提供结构分析、基础设计及土木工程服务，同时遵循泰国标准与国际规范。

02
Technical Consulting技术咨询

Advice on specs, material selection, and compliance with Thai building regulations. We're useful from the early design stage right through to sign-off.就规范、材料选型及泰国建筑法规合规性提供专业建议，从初步设计到竣工验收全程支持。

03
MEP Engineering机电管道工程

Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing design for mid-to-large projects. We coordinate MEP systems alongside the structural and architectural teams.为中大型项目设计机械、电气及管道系统，并与结构及建筑团队协调配合，避免现场冲突。

04
Construction Supervision施工监督

On-site supervision to make sure work matches the approved drawings. We flag issues early, run QA checks, and report progress to all parties.现场监督施工，确保工程符合批准图纸，提前发现问题，执行质量检查并定期汇报进展。

05
Document Translation技术文件翻译

Translation of shop drawings, BOQs, contracts, and inspection reports between Thai, Chinese, and English — especially useful for sino-Thai joint ventures.专业翻译施工图、工程量清单、合同及检测报告，特别适用于泰中合资项目，确保沟通准确无误。

06
Bilingual Site Liaison双语现场联络

Thai–Chinese–English interpreters embedded in your project team. They handle real-time communication between contractors, local subs, and government offices.泰中英三语口译员驻场，负责承包商、本地分包商及政府部门之间的实时沟通协调。

Three Languages In-House三语团队

Thai, Chinese, and English — all in-house. This cuts out the miscommunication that often slows cross-border projects down.团队内部具备泰语、中文及英语能力，有效避免跨国项目中因语言障碍造成的沟通延误。

Clear Timelines清晰的工期管理

We work to specific milestones and keep clients updated regularly. If something needs adjusting, we say so early rather than letting it become a problem.按明确里程碑推进，定期向客户汇报进展。如需调整，我们会提前告知，而非等问题发酵。

We agree on scope and fees before starting. Clients know exactly what they're paying for — no surprises part-way through a project.合作前明确服务范围与费用，客户清楚了解所付费用的对应服务，项目过程中无隐性收费。
